# Micro-SaaS exits (QR / link shorteners) and primary SaaS benchmarks

Observed small QR/link tools sit at roughly $50-$900 MRR (TrustMRR, Stripe-verified) with a few self-reported at $600-$4k MRR; the two Flippa QR sales found sold (price hidden) with listing multiples of 1.8x-2.4x profit. ## What monthly churn and free-to-paid conversion do primary sources document for products under $25/month?

### Takeaway
ChartMogul's own data: <$10 ARPA churns 6-7% monthly, $500+ 1-2%. Freemium-to-paid median is ~5% (OpenView, via secondary summary); no primary sub-$25 price-point churn figure from Paddle/ProfitWell was accessible.

### Cited Findings
- ChartMogul help center: "Businesses with less than $10 ARPA typically see 6-7% monthly churn"; "$500 ARPA or more typically see 1-2% monthly churn." No sample size on that article; page "last updated Oct 22, 2026" (revision date, not data date). — [ChartMogul](https://help.chartmogul.com/hc/en-us/articles/203359321)
- ChartMogul describes its Benchmarks as drawn from 2,500+ SaaS companies' real revenue data (search summary). — [ChartMogul blog](https://chartmogul.com/blog/compare-your-performance-to-the-market-with-chartmogul-benchmarks/)
- OpenView 2023 Product Benchmarks: median freemium free-to-paid ~5% within 6 months (dev tools cut, per Boldstart summary); 2022 report: 5% freemium vs 17% free trial (per Malpani Ventures summary). Secondary, confirm against the OpenView PDF. — [Boldstart](https://boldstart.vc/?p=1596); [Malpani](https://www.malpaniventures.com/blog/learnings-from-open-view-s-product-benchmarks-report-2022)
- ChartMogul product page 14% free-to-paid / 24-day median is an illustrative sample table, not a benchmark. — [ChartMogul](https://chartmogul.com/product-led-growth/)
- ProfitWell/Paddle: SMB monthly churn guidance 2.5-5% (via Lenny's summary, secondary); annual SMB churn 14-22% attributed to Paddle/ProfitWell by a blog (unverified). — [Lenny's](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/monthly-churn-benchmarks); [ProfitWell](https://profitwell.com/blog/average-revenue-churn-rate-benchmarks)
- Flippa QR listing self-reports 6% churn (see above), consistent with the ChartMogul <$10 ARPA band.

### Inferences
- A $3-$25/mo QR product should plan on 4-7% monthly logo churn; combined with ~5% freemium conversion, this supports conservative scenarios.

### Gaps
- No primary Paddle/ProfitWell churn-by-price-point numbers (Lenny post paywalled; ProfitWell PDF not fetched). No ChartMogul free-to-paid benchmark by ARPA. OpenView original PDF not fetched. ChartMogul benchmark sample year unknown.

## What gross margin and CAC-payback medians do the primary surveys report?

### Takeaway
KeyBanc/Sapphire 2022: median ~23-month CAC payback, 77% subscription / 73% blended gross margin, 14% gross churn. Newer editions not obtained, and these are venture-scale companies, not micro-SaaS.

### Cited Findings
- KeyBanc 2022 survey via Kellblog: gross churn 14%, subscription gross margin 77%, blended 73%. — [Kellblog](https://kellblog.com/2022/10/30/key-takeaways-from-the-2022-keybanc-saas-metrics-survey/)
- Sapphire page describes median ~23-month CAC payback in 2022 and gross magic number 0.7. — [Sapphire](https://sapphireventures.com/press/keybanc-capital-markets-and-sapphire-ventures-private-saas-company-survey)
- SaaS Capital 2025 retention brief: GRR = Dec-2024 MRR from Dec-2023 customers excluding expansion / Dec-2023 MRR; median NRR $25-50k ACV band 102% (top quartile 111%, bottom 97%); growth median 24% (>$1M ARR). Sample size not stated on page. — [SaaS Capital](https://www.saas-capital.com/research/saas-retention-benchmarks-for-private-b2b-companies/)
- SaaS Capital (search summary): 2023 median GRR 91%, NRR 102%; ACV <$25k median GRR 90%; bootstrapped $3M-$20M ARR median GRR 91% (2026 data), NRR 103%. Annual, not monthly, churn. — [SaaS Capital](https://www.saas-capital.com/benchmarking/)
- Aleph x Benchmarkit 2026 (secondary via techsy.io, unverified): median GRR fell 88% to 84% in 2025 across 342 companies.

### Inferences
- ~10% annual revenue churn (SaaS Capital) is B2B, higher ACV; it is not applicable to $5-$20/mo consumer-ish QR plans (which see ~50%+ annual logo churn per ChartMogul).

### Gaps
- 2023-2025 KeyBanc/Sapphire PDF medians not accessed; gross-margin medians for micro-SaaS not found; 14-15% / 37-month figures from a third-party site are unverified and excluded.

## What ARR multiples apply to very small SaaS exits (2024-2026)?

### Takeaway
Sub-$100k ARR SaaS is priced on seller profit (SDE), typically 2-3x, not ARR; this matches the 1.8x-2.4x Flippa listings. Larger private SaaS median revenue multiple ~3.8x in 2025.

### Cited Findings
- Guide (secondary): under $100K ARR priced on SDE at 2.0x-3.0x, buyers individual operators; under $500K ARR 2-3x SDE. — [Livmo](https://livmo.com/blog/micro-saas-valuation/)
- Aventis Advisors (secondary): private SaaS median 3.8x revenue in 2025, from 2.9x trough in 2024. — via [Fungies summary](https://fungies.io/saas-valuation-statistics-2026-4/)
- bigideasdb "651 listings" study of small-SaaS multiples could not be fetched (HTTP 429). — [bigideasdb](https://bigideasdb.com/state-of-saas-valuations-2026)

### Inferences
- Plausible exit for a $10k ARR, ~98%-margin tool: roughly $15k-$30k (1.5x-3x profit, which is also 1.5x-3x ARR here); an unsupported-by-primary-data estimate.

### Gaps
- No primary acquire.com, Flippa or Empire Flippers published multiples report fetched; no sold (vs asking) multiples verified.
