# Market size, demand and growth for QR codes and dynamic-QR / QR-analytics software (2025-2026)

Note on method: search-snippet and page-fetch based. Many research-firm pages are paywalled or reachable only via reposts/listings; where I could not open the primary page I say so. I did NOT obtain primary pages from Grand View, MarketsandMarkets (QR generator), Fortune Business Insights, Statista (figures masked), Verified Market Research or Mordor (my fetch of the Mordor page 404'd; its numbers are via secondary vendor blogs). No dynamic-QR-only market estimate from a named top-tier firm was found.

Scope definition, not methodology quality, explains most of the 10x+ spread; none of the figures is verifiable from a primary methodology page. ## 2. Scanning volume and adoption statistics (and vendor bias)

### Takeaway
Independent consumer data (eMarketer/Statista, TEAM LEWIS survey, GS1 US survey) shows ~95-100M US smartphone QR scanners and roughly two-thirds of US adults scanning yearly; "scans per year" global totals are not credibly measured, and nearly all volume figures come from vendors (biased upward and platform-specific).

### Cited Findings
Independent / third-party

- Statista page "US smartphone users who used a QR code scanner 2020-2025": exact numbers masked/paywalled in what I saw — [Statista](https://www.statista.com/statistics/1297768/qr-code-users-usa/). ## 3. Growth drivers: GS1 Sunrise 2027, packaging, payments, menus, attribution

### Takeaway
GS1 Sunrise 2027 is a retailer/industry initiative (not law) to get POS scanners reading 2D barcodes (QR with GS1 Digital Link, or DataMatrix) carrying the GTIN; it creates a managed-link (resolver) demand for brands and a risk/opportunity for generators that do not support GS1 Digital Link.

### Cited Findings
- GS1 US: Sunrise 2027 = global retail transition from 1D UPC to 2D barcodes such as QR codes; page gives no firm legal deadline, frames 2027 as milestone and a "Crawl-Walk-Run" path (install 2D-capable scanners; add 2D to private label; optimise traceability/inventory). Advises updating existing QR codes to GS1 Digital Link including the GTIN so one code works at checkout and for consumer info — [GS1 US Sunrise 2027](https://www.gs1us.org/industries-and-insights/by-topic/sunrise-2027)
- GS1 US 2025 consumer survey: 79% more likely to buy products with a scannable QR code giving extra info; 77% say product info is important; 62% willing to pay more for detailed product info (note: GS1 is an interested party) — [GS1 US](https://www.gs1us.org/industries-and-insights/by-topic/sunrise-2027)
- Vendor/industry explainers state the common reading: by 31 Dec 2027 retail POS should be able to read a GTIN in a 2D barcode; both GS1 DataMatrix and GS1 Digital Link QR are supported; 1D codes are not being phased out by Sunrise; not a legal mandate — [Dynamsoft](https://www.dynamsoft.com/blog/insights/gs1-sunrise-2027-transition-to-2d-barcodes-for-global-commerce/); [Videojet](https://www.videojet.com/us/homepage/resources/learn/gs1-sunrise-2027.html) (secondary, vendor); I did not retrieve GS1's Implementation Guideline directly
- GS1 progress figures via QRCodeChimp (secondary): 48 countries testing 2D barcodes representing ~88% of global GDP (2024); 2020 GS1 US research: 82% of retailers and 92% of brand owners supported moving beyond UPC; 90% of POS scanning solutions needed before linear barcodes can be dropped (Implementation Guideline) — [QRCodeChimp](https://qrcodechimp.com/qr-code-statistics)
- Payments: see Juniper/RBI/METI figures in Section 2; Mordor via blogs says payments/transactions = 45.19% of application revenue (2025), marketing/advertising is the fastest-growing application (18.16% CAGR) — [QRCodeChimp](https://qrcodechimp.com/qr-code-statistics)
- Menus: 48% of TEAM LEWIS respondents used QR for menus; Scanova: hospitality menu codes = 17.7% of deployments but 43.4% of scans (avg 5,076 scans) — [Scanova](https://scanova.io/blog/qr-code-statistics/)
- Travel: 60% of travellers used mobile boarding passes (SITA 2024) — [Scanova](https://scanova.io/blog/qr-code-statistics/)
- Packaging/CPG (Scanova platform): CPG = 46.9M of 69.9M scans (67%); custom landing pages 9.7% of deployments but 48.9% of scans; manufacturing spec sheets 18.5% of codes but 2.9% of scans — [Scanova](https://scanova.io/blog/qr-code-statistics/)

### Inferences
- Sunrise 2027 matters to qrwty mainly if it supports GS1 Digital Link URL structures (GTIN-based path routing, resolver-style redirects); this is a feature gap to check, not an automatic demand tailwind. Big brands will likely use GS1-member/resolver services, so the SMB/CPG long tail is the plausible niche.
- Scanova's CPG skew is a single-vendor customer mix, not market share.

### Gaps
- No quantified estimate of how many SKUs/brands will migrate to GS1 Digital Link or spend on it; GS1 primary implementation guideline not retrieved; Bitly/attribution-specific data not found.
