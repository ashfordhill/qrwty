### Redirect service

- **Lookup and status codes:** look up host and slug through Q06's least-privilege route view.
  Answer 302 with `no-store`. Support HEAD with no event. Use 404, 403, 410 and 503 as the contract
  defines them.
- **Cache:** Valkey cache with a fixed validity deadline on every hit, bounded calls and miss
  coalescing.
- **Click events:** publish a sanitized click event with a stable ID on Pub/Sub, and **await its
  ACK before the 302**. A failed or unknown ACK means 503 without a `Location` header.
- **Isolation:** no identity or payment dependency.
- **Diagram setting:** the diagram's 1000 ms Redirect request timeout is shorter than the
  publisher's 2000 ms bound. Keep the diagram unchanged and set the publisher's deadline under the
  request timeout in the stub. Record this.

### Analytics worker

- **Consume and commit:** consume the click subscription. The name follows QG28:
  `click-events--analytics-worker--analytics-worker-click-aggregator`.
- **Dedup before ACK:** do the atomic dedup and the aggregate commit before the ACK. Quarantine bad
  events.
- **Archive:** archive subscription settings as Q04 and Q13 configured them.
- **Owner analytics:** read routes for the owner, such as `GET /api/v1/me/links/{id}/analytics` and
  the CSV export, as the contract names them. They show counts, freshness and delay. Bot GETs are
  recorded but filtered out of the counts. No raw IP, full URL or cookies are stored.
