### Screens, wired to A's live API

Use the routes A3 and A4 built (Session API's `/api/v1/…` and `/auth/…`, with the CSRF token from
`/api/v1/session`).
- **Create.** A guest or a signed-in user enters a long link and presses Shorten. The UI shows the
  normal error states, such as invalid destination, Web Risk refusal and too many new links.
- **Result:**
  - the short link, with Copy and Open;
  - a QR code, with Download PNG and SVG, generated in the browser;
  - Save to my links, which runs sign-in and then the claim.
- **Sign in / Sign out,** through `/auth/login?returnTo=…` and `/auth/logout`.
- **My links:**
  - a cursor-paged list;
  - change a link's destination, with If-Match and a stale-edit message;
  - disable and delete a link;
  - each link's click analytics and its CSV export.
- **Account:** "Plans and billing" shows the current plan, which is free for now (A7 adds Stripe).
  It also offers delete-my-account.

### Serving

Serve the app from the existing `qrwty-edge` Worker (`deploy/cloudflare/`) as static assets.
- **Paths.** qrwty.io/ is the app. Keep `/versions/` and everything it links. Use asset paths that
  A4's slug routing already reserves (`/app/`, `/assets/`). The placeholder's "Versions: what
  happened" link moves into the app's footer.
- **Security headers.** Update the Worker's CSP for app pages only: `script-src 'self'` and
  `connect-src 'self'`, with no inline script and no external origins. Keep S01's other headers.
- **Fonts.** Self-host Nunito (SIL OFL) or fall back to the system font. No Google Fonts request.
- **Dependencies.** Use no framework unless it clearly saves code. A pinned QR library at least two
  weeks old is fine. The prototype's `qr.js` may already be enough.

### Deploy

Roll out with `QRWTY_REDEPLOY_DIR=.scratch/A5/redeploy infra/gcp/scripts/redeploy.sh HEAD`.

Then run a live smoke in headless Chromium, by mouse only, at desktop 1440×900 and phone 360×800:
1. Create a link.
2. Copy it, and check the clipboard.
3. Download the QR code, and decode it.
4. Sign in as A3's test user, through Auth0 Universal Login.
5. Save the link to My links.
6. Edit its destination.
7. Open the link's analytics. They show the click from opening the short link.
8. Sign out.

Check light and dark themes, check that there is no horizontal scroll at 360 px, and look for 0
console errors.
